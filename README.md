## About me

- Software engineer at **Buzlin Holdings Inc**, working on Buzlin (a marketplace and service-booking platform) and BuzRyde (a ride-hailing service in Canada).
- Founder of **[Teevexa Ltd](https://www.teevexa.com)**, a software development company that delivers custom software, AI agents and production-ready apps for businesses.
- Trained as a **chemical engineer**, which taught me to look at a business like a process: inputs, outputs, bottlenecks and the points where things quietly go wrong.
- I start with the business problem, recommend only what is needed, and build solutions that keep working after launch.
